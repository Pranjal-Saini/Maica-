Why it exists

The expensive bugs live between two people.

A script fires on a record whose configuration changed months ago. Neither specialist can see the other half, so the investigation becomes two people taking turns being certain it isn't their side.

Functional consultant

Sees configuration, not code

Knows the account, the forms, the approval routing and what finance expects. Cannot read the SuiteScript that ran at 09:15.

Technical consultant

Sees code, not configuration

Knows exactly what the script does. Doesn't know the posting account was reassigned three months ago, or by whom.

MAICA

Reads both halves and ranks what it finds

One timeline across configuration changes, scripts, workflows and users — ordered by strength of evidence, with every factor traceable to the exact row it came from.

How it works

Start with what you can already export.

No administrator, no integration record, no IT ticket. If you can run a saved search, you can start in five minutes.

01

Export

A saved-search CSV and a System Notes export — the things already on your screen. Messy exports are fine; MAICA reports what it understood and what it skipped.

02

Upload

Drop the files in together. Each one's type is read from its own column headers, normalised into a single shape and stored with its provenance intact.

03

Ask about a transaction

Give it the transaction that posted wrong. You get contributing factors ranked by support, the evidence behind each, and what could not be checked.

What it claims

It never hands you a single verdict.

A component touching a field does not prove it caused the outcome. Every factor carries the strength of its own evidence, in four words that always mean the same thing.

Confirmed

The change itself is documented in the evidence. Not a claim about cause.

Likely

Strongly supported by chronology and direct record relationships, but not proven.

Uncertain

Evidence exists and important gaps remain. The gaps are stated, not glossed.

Insufficient evidence

No reliable conclusion available from what was uploaded. Said plainly.

Questions

What people ask first.

Does MAICA connect to my NetSuite account?

Not today. You upload exports you generate yourself, so nothing touches your account and no administrator has to approve anything. A live read-only connection is planned as an upgrade for people who already want it, never as a requirement to start.

Can it change anything in my account?

No, and not as a matter of policy. There is no code in MAICA that writes to NetSuite. Even the planned live connection is read-only, requesting no permission the analysis does not use.

What exactly do I upload?

A saved-search CSV of the transactions you care about, and a System Notes export covering the same period. Both are things a consultant can already produce without special rights.

Extra columns, renamed headers and partial date ranges are all fine. MAICA reports how many rows it understood and how many it skipped, and why.

Will it tell me the root cause?

Deliberately not. It returns contributing factors ranked by the strength of their evidence, and you decide. A script touching a field does not prove it caused the outcome, and a tool that says otherwise is guessing with confidence.

What happens to my client's data?

It is stored so the analysis can cite it, isolated per client account, and treated as confidential regardless of what it contains. You can delete a client account and everything uploaded under it at any time.

The details are in the privacy policy.

Who is it for?

NetSuite consultants — functional and technical — and the administrators and controllers who feel the pain when a transaction posts wrong. It is built for the gap between the two specialisms, so it is most useful when neither person can see the whole picture alone.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the beta. Pricing is not settled, and when it is, it will be aimed at the client or the consulting firm rather than at the individual consultant — the person who saves the time is rarely the person who captures the benefit.

How far along is it?

Beta, and honest about it. Upload, normalisation, the dependency graph, the ranking and the report all work end to end. The live NetSuite connection does not exist yet, and some evidence types are not ingested because their real column names have not been verified against a genuine export.
