## Product Rules

- The output is a **ranked map of contributing factors**, never a single verdict.
  The consultant decides; the tool shows them where to look.
- The product exists in the gap between functional consultants (see configuration,
  not code) and technical consultants (see code, not configuration). Any feature
  that only serves one side is a feature for a tool that already exists.
- Primary output shape: contributing factors ranked by support, the evidence behind
  each, what could not be checked, and the next thing worth looking at.
- The consultant is the champion, not the payer. The end client or the consulting
  firm pays. Do not build self-serve consumer pricing, per-seat consultant billing,
  or anything that assumes the consultant swipes a card.
- Two open questions drive priority: does the analysis generalise across
  differently-customised accounts, and will anyone pay. Prefer work that answers
  one of them over work that does neither.
